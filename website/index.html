Answers you can stand behind.

EchoIdeal listens to both sides of your interview and suggests what to say next, built from your CV, the job description and the stories you prepared. When it doesn’t know something about you, it tells you instead of making it up.

§ The idea

Most AI copilots will put words in your mouth. This one starts from yours.

An answer is only useful if you can defend it in the follow-up. So EchoIdeal separates what it knows about you from what it’s guessing, and it tells you which is which.

  1. I.

    Grounded in your documents

    Anything it says about you, from your roles to your projects to your numbers, comes from your Knowledge Documents or from what’s already been said in the Meeting.

  2. II.

    Honest about the gaps

    If a question needs a fact it doesn’t have, like a salary or a start date, the answer is marked Not from your knowledge. You fill in the blank. It doesn’t.

  3. III.

    Consistent with what you said

    Say something that contradicts your CV and you get a private Discrepancy note. Later answers follow what you actually said, so your story holds together.

02 During the interview

Keeps up with both sides of the conversation.

EchoIdeal hears the Interviewer through your system audio and you through your microphone, and keeps a running memory of the Meeting, so every Suggested Answer fits what’s already been said.

System audio is the Interviewer and your microphone is you. Voice activity detection keeps the transcript live.

Merge overlapping intervals

Given a list of intervals [start, end], merge every overlapping pair and return the intervals that cover all the input.

in:  [[1,3],[2,6],[8,10],[15,18]]
out: [[1,6],[8,10],[15,18]]
Screen Capture: Click and drag to select area · Press ESC to cancel
Assessment · SolutionWeb Search on
def merge(intervals):
  intervals.sort()
  out = []
  for s, e in intervals:
    if out and s <= out[-1][1]:
      out[-1][1] = max(out[-1][1], e)
    else:
      out.append([s, e])
  return out

Sort by start, then sweep once: O(n log n) time.

§ Privacy & control

Invisible to the call. Open to inspection.

Stealth isn’t the point of EchoIdeal, but your preparation is nobody else’s business. The overlay stays out of screen shares, nothing joins your meeting, and every line of code is public.

  • Hidden from screen shares

    The overlay floats above your other apps, but it isn’t captured in screen shares or recordings.

  • No bot joins the call

    EchoIdeal listens to your computer’s audio. Nothing is added to the meeting’s participant list.

  • History stays with you

    Meetings, Recaps and history are stored locally in SQLite, on your machine.

  • Your model, your key

    Bring your own API key, or point it at a local model through Ollama.

§ FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Something we haven’t covered? Once the GitHub repository is public, which is coming soon, you’ll be able to open an issue there.

Is EchoIdeal really free?

Yes. EchoIdeal is open-source software under the GPL-3.0 licence. There’s no subscription and no account with us. You bring your own API key for the model provider you choose and pay them directly for what you use, or run a local model through Ollama.

What does “Not from your knowledge” mean?

It marks an Ungrounded Answer. When a question needs a fact about you (a date, a number, a project, a salary) that isn’t in your Active Knowledge or anything said in the Meeting, the Suggested Answer carries that marker so you can check it before you say it.

General, technical and world questions are never marked. Your documents don’t limit what the AI knows about the world.

What if I say something that contradicts my CV?

You’ll see a private Discrepancy note, like “You said almost five years; your CV says 2023 – present.” From then on, Suggested Answers follow what you actually said, so you don’t contradict yourself twice. You can correct yourself out loud, or carry on consistently. That’s your call.

Can the other side see EchoIdeal?

The overlay is hidden from screen shares and recordings, and no bot joins your call, because EchoIdeal listens to your computer’s audio rather than connecting to the meeting.

Whether using an assistant is appropriate depends on you and the rules of the conversation you’re in. EchoIdeal is built to help you present your real experience clearly, not to invent a new one.

Where does my data go?

Your Meetings, Recaps and history are stored locally in SQLite. To transcribe and answer, audio and questions go to the speech-to-text and AI providers you configure, along with your Active Knowledge. A Searched Document sends only its best-matching passages. If you’d rather not use a cloud AI provider, choose a local model through Ollama.

Which models can I use?

OpenAI, Claude, Gemini and Grok, plus custom and local providers such as Ollama. Searched Documents also need an embeddings provider, which is optional.

Does it help with coding tests?

Yes. Start an Assessment, take a Screen Capture of the problem, and the Suggested Answer is a solution to submit. Switch on Web Search if you want documentation to inform it. Afterwards, the Assessment Recap lists each problem and your solution.

Which platforms does it run on?

Windows, macOS and Linux. Builds are published on GitHub Releases. If there isn’t one for your platform yet, the README explains how to build from source with Node.js and Rust.

Tell your own story, well.

Free and open source. Bring your own key or run a local model. Your history stays on your machine.
